About Raja Watches

Built on one idea — a good watch shouldn't shout.

Raja Watches started as a small, personal collection before it became a shop. Every piece here was chosen the same way: held, worn for a week, judged on how it feels — not just how it photographs.

About me: I'm the person behind Raja Watches — sourcing, testing, and packing every order myself. No call centers, no middlemen. If you message, you're talking to the person who picked the watch.

Rose gold and black aren't just a color scheme here — it's the standard every piece in the collection is measured against.
